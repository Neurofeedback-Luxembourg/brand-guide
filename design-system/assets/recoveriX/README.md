# recoveriX

Images for **recoveriX Luxembourg**, the BCI (brain-computer interface / interface cerveau-ordinateur) neurorehabilitation service that Neurofeedback Luxembourg runs as the exclusive g.tec franchise for Luxembourg. Added 2026-10-24 from files provided by François Altwies.

## Rules

- **Always name and label it recoveriX** (lower-case r, capital X), and "recoveriX Luxembourg" when the local service is meant. Keep it visibly separate from Neurofeedback Luxembourg's own training offer (Offer C in the main README).
- **Wording:** g.tec's intended purpose — motor neurorehabilitation after stroke and in multiple sclerosis. No outcome promises.
- **Colour:** recoveriX green `#00974D` for recoveriX labels and accents (recoveriX has no design system of its own).
- **Contact and link:** https://neurofeedback-luxembourg.recoverix.com/fr/ · +352 42 31 55 55.
- **Source and rights:** official images from g.tec medical engineering. Neurofeedback Luxembourg is the official and exclusive recoveriX franchise owner for Luxembourg and uses them freely in its research and marketing (confirmed by François Altwies, 2026-10-24).
