# Logos

The Gemini-drawn low-poly brain, adopted as primary on 2026-09-24. PNG, 900 px wide (icon 512 px); 4096 px masters in GitHub brand-guide/assets/logos/primary.

- `logo-transparent.png` — dark text, transparent; on white or lilac.
- `logo-white.png` — on a white box; use as the badge on the gradient.
- `logo-reversed-transparent.png` — white text; directly on the gradient or slate.
- `logo-icon-transparent.png` — brain only; avatars, favicons, small formats.
- `logo-site.png` — the website header logo: horizontal, white brain and "Neurofeedback-Luxembourg.com" text, transparent; only on the gradient (site header, social image top band). Copied from neurofeedback-luxembourg.com.

- `logo-transparent.svg` — vector full logo, dark text, transparent; print and large sizes. Approved 2026-10-21.
- `logo-icon-transparent.svg` — vector brain only; print and large sizes. Approved 2026-10-21.

Clear space: at least the height of the capital "N" in the wordmark on every side (icon alone: a quarter of its width). Minimum size: full logo 30 mm / 120 px wide; icon 8 mm / 24 px wide.

Never redraw, trace, recolour or regenerate the logo. The two SVGs are the one approved redraw of the 4096 px master; use them as they are.
