Neurofeedback Luxembourg — design system

Brand rules, colours, type, logos, photos, recoveriX images and components, as plain files. Any agent or person can read this page and every file in it without a login.

For AI agents (ChatGPT, Gemini, Codex, Mistral, Claude...): start with the plain-text index llms.txt, which lists every file with a direct link. Then:
  1. README.md — the brand book: voice, main slogan, SEO keywords, colours, logo, imagery, official accounts.
  2. tokens.json / tokens.css — every colour, type style, spacing step and radius.
  3. The README of the asset group you need, then the files as they are. Never redraw the logo.
Folder on GitHub: https://github.com/Neurofeedback-Luxembourg/brand-guide/tree/main/design-system

Colours

  • white#ffffffPage ground. The brand is light-mode only (Brand Guide v2.1.3 §11.2).
  • ink#090909All body text, on white (19.91:1) and lilac (13.99:1). Never a brand colour for paragraphs.
  • lilac#e2d4e2Tinted surfaces: cards, section bands. Never text on white.
  • plum#813e68Logo colour; headings or large accents on white (7.44:1).
  • deep-blue#074d79Logo colour; headings or accents on white (8.95:1) and lilac (6.29:1).
  • plum-deep#5d2c4cGradient start; data callouts and headings on white (10.88:1).
  • slate#2f5673Gradient end; secondary text on white (7.78:1).
  • magenta#9f016bLinks, small accents, active states and the primary button fill. On white 7.80:1, on lilac 5.48:1. Never paragraphs, never error.
  • link#9f016bLinks — alias of magenta.
  • on-brand#ffffffText on the gradient and on any brand-colour fill (7.44–10.88:1).
  • success#16a34aSuccess icons, borders, large text only (3.30:1 on white).
  • success-text#15803dSuccess text at normal size (5.02:1 on white).
  • warning#d97706Warning icons, borders, large text only (3.19:1).
  • warning-text#b45309Warning text at normal size (5.02:1).
  • error#dc2626Error icons and borders (4.83:1).
  • error-text#b91c1cError text (6.47:1). Never use magenta for errors.
  • muted#4a4150Secondary text: bylines, sources, captions. On white 9.71:1, on lilac 6.82:1.
  • lilac-soft#f4edf4Inner panel inside a lilac or white card (guess slider). Ink on it 17.32:1.
  • rule#cdb9cdHairlines and the empty part of mini-visuals. Decorative only (1.3:1 on lilac): never the only carrier of meaning.

Components

Plain HTML/CSS classes (prefix nfl-) in components/bundle.css; load tokens.css first.

  • AboutFigure The expandable "About this figure" / « À propos de ce chiffre » detail: caveats, population, period and the full source with page reference. preview · guidelines
  • Button A single clear action; the primary one is usually the free, non-binding phone call. preview · guidelines
  • Card A lilac surface that groups one idea: a service, a step, a question. preview · guidelines
  • ChartFrame A diagram with everything that travels with it: title, share menu, the SVG chart, a one-line takeaway with its source, "About this figure" for method notes, "Show as table" with the data, and the brand line. preview · guidelines
  • ClaimTag The claim-ladder label (Brand Guide §5) on every figure, study and opinion: Fact · Research finding · Our observation · Opinion. preview · guidelines
  • Cover preview
  • DataCallout A verified number with one short label. preview · guidelines
  • Disclaimer Required at the end of every health piece (web, social, slides, print). preview · guidelines
  • FigureCard One sourced number as a card: claim tag, share menu, the big number, one confident sentence, a mini-visual, the "About this figure" detail and the brand line. preview · guidelines
  • GuessSlider "Guess first" on a section's headline figure: the visitor drags a slider (0–100 %), then the real number animates in with the gap to the guess ("You guessed 40%. The real figure is 36%, 4 points lower."). preview · guidelines
  • Hero The page or slide opener: the gradient with white text and the logo badge top-right. preview · guidelines
  • LanguageSwitch Globe icon with "EN · FR"; the current language is highlighted, the other is a link to the same page in that language. preview · guidelines
  • MythStrip "Myth or number?" / « Idée reçue ou chiffre ? »: a short strip between sections that states a common belief and reveals the answer from a figure already on the page. preview · guidelines
  • ShareMenu Plain share links on every card and chart: LinkedIn, Facebook, X, WhatsApp, email and "Copy link". No third-party share scripts. preview · guidelines
  • SiteFooter Footer of the statistics site: imprint, address, phone, privacy link and the health disclaimer. preview · guidelines
  • SiteHeader The lean branded header of the statistics site (data.neurofeedback-luxembourg.com): logo, four links to the main site, the free-call button and the language switch. preview · guidelines
  • StatusNote A short state message (form sent, slot full, error). preview · guidelines
