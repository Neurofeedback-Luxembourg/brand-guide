What share of employees in Luxembourg is at moderate or high risk of burnout?

36%

More than one employee in three is at moderate or high risk of burnout, up from 16% in 2014.

About this figure

Quality of Work Index 2025 (n = 3,171). The 2024 value is lower because the order of the questionnaire changed that year; CSL advises against comparing with 2024.

Source: Chambre des salariés (CSL), University of Luxembourg, infas, CSL summary of results (PDF, FR) (p. 10)

Chambre des salariés (CSL) data.neurofeedback-luxembourg.com/work/
