Usage Guidelines

Follow these guidelines to maintain brand consistency and integrity.

✓
Use logos on backgrounds with sufficient contrast
Ensure logos are clearly visible against their background.
✓
Maintain minimum clear space around logos
Keep a margin equal to the height of the brain icon around all logos.
✓
Use vector formats (SVG) whenever possible
SVG files scale perfectly at any size without quality loss.
✗
Don't stretch, distort, or modify logo proportions
Always maintain the original aspect ratio when resizing.
✗
Don't change brand colors or add effects
Use logos as provided without gradients, shadows, or color modifications.
✗
Don't place logos on busy or distracting backgrounds
Ensure backgrounds allow the logo to be the focal point.
