**1\. Positioning**

For high-achievers and people seeking sustainable mental wellness, Neurofeedback Luxembourg is Luxembourg's measurement-first brain training.  
**We do not guess. We measure, we train, we re-measure.**

### **Four differentiators**

> 1. **Measurement-first.** Training begins with a 19-electrode qEEG brain map. 3,500+ scans since 2014\. The protocol follows the data, not a template.  
> 2. **Self-regulation, not dependence.** The client's brain does the work. The equipment is the mirror. That is what makes results durable.  
> 3. **In our offices and at home.** Training in our offices, plus devices clients can rent or buy to continue at home.  
> 4. **Research-active.** We are an approved private research institute and host CURATOR, a research project funded by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR Industrial Fellowship) — brain-curator.org. This is a trust factor, never an offer and never evidence that a service works for a given person.

### **Public wording**

Internally we say “clinic”. Publicly we speak of **our offices** (FR and DE equivalents: to be confirmed by François). Never “clinic”, “medical centre” or “therapy centre” in public copy.

### **The competitive reality**

Reimbursed providers compete on out-of-pocket cost. We do not win on price and must never try. We win on measurement and durability. Every piece of communication should reinforce that, never drift toward price competition.

## ---

**2\. What we offer — three categories, never merged**

### **A. Training services (in our offices)**

> 1. **qEEG / BrainMap** — a 19-electrode measurement that informs a training plan. Never a diagnosis.  
> 2. **Neurofeedback training**  
> 3. **Safe and Sound Protocol (SSP)** — a listening programme.  
> 4. **Standard package** — includes transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation (tVNS) and photobiomodulation (PBM) sessions alongside neurofeedback.

**Client pathway (owner-confirmed by François, 2026-09-24):** first step is a free, non-binding phone call; neurofeedback training is a minimum of 10 sessions, once a week.

### **B. Products (sold and rented)**

PBM and tVNS devices for use at home, e.g. Vielight Duo (light) and Vielight Vagus (vagus nerve stimulation).

### **C. recoveriX Luxembourg — partner device programme**

We run the exclusive g.tec franchise **recoveriX Luxembourg**. Its own rules apply:

> * Follow g.tec's franchise brand rules (design-system tokens/brands/recoverix): recoveriX spelled with a capital X, its own visual identity.  
> * recoveriX is a CE-marked medical device. Rehabilitation language is allowed **only** on recoveriX pages and materials, and only within g.tec's stated intended purpose. Before new recoveriX marketing, confirm the current certificate, intended purpose and price (open item in the README).  
> * Never mix recoveriX clinical wording into Neurofeedback Luxembourg training copy, and never imply neurofeedback shares recoveriX's medical-device status.

### **Not an offer: research**

The research institute status and the CURATOR project build trust. Mention them as “we also conduct research”, never as a service, and never cite research as proof that training works for an individual.

### **Describing mechanisms**

We deliberately do not communicate with clinical precision. Permitted vocabulary: **sound** (SSP) · **light** (PBM) · **vagus nerve stimulation** (tVNS) · **measurement** (qEEG) · **feedback** (neurofeedback). Do not name device protocols or elaborate further. The imprecision is a deliberate editorial choice, not a gap to fill in.  
Product and device claims are a separate regulatory surface from training services and depend on each device's classification. Never state “CE-certified” for PBM or tVNS devices until confirmed per model. The banned-words list applies in full either way (recoveriX pages excepted as above).

**3\. Voice**

| Attribute | What it means | What it is not |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Authoritative | Cite mechanism and evidence. State what is known and what is not. | Arrogant, dismissive, jargon-heavy |
| Empowering | The client's brain does the work; we provide the mirror. | Guru-like, dependency-creating |
| Precise | Specific numbers, specific protocols, specific language. | Vague wellness language |
| Safe | Non-invasive and painless, stated plainly and never overstated. | Absolute safety claims |

### **Signature moves**

> * Mechanism before benefit, always — and explain the mechanism with a metaphor (see §4).  
> * One concrete verified number per piece — 3,500+ scans · 20,000+ sessions · 19 electrodes · since 2014 · 120+ five-star reviews.  
> * Close with agency, not urgency. Default invitation: the free, non-binding phone call. No countdown timers, no scarcity, no fear.  
> * Name the uncertainty. Separate research findings, hypotheses and individual experience.  
> * Never invent statistics, references, testimonials, prices or outcome timelines.

**Authority figures:** the numbers above are canonical. Other figures (2,000 / 2,500 / 3,000 scans; founded 2013\) still appear in older drafts and must be corrected to match this file.

### **Banned words — non-negotiable (merged with the website compliance list)**

**EN:** cure · heal · treat · treatment (outside clinical/regulatory context and recoveriX pages) · patient (use client) · diagnose / diagnosis / diagnostic · clinically proven · guaranteed · miracle · revolutionary · risk-free · without risk · zero side effects / no side effects · 100% safe · energy healing · alternative to medication · unlock your potential · fix · disorder · sufferer · life-changing (permitted only inside a direct client quote)  
**FR:** traitement · traiter · soigner · guérir · guérison · patient · diagnostic · diagnostiquer · cliniquement prouvé · sans effets secondaires · sans risque  
**Additionally on condition-topic pages** (ADHD, depression, anxiety, autism, sleep, migraines, trauma/PTSD): evidence-based · clinical · biomarkers (FR: fondé sur les preuves · clinique · biomarqueurs). Describing the CURATOR research project is not a condition page.  
**Use instead:** training · sessions · program · support · help with · client · assessment · brain map · based on published research · non-invasive and painless (FR: entraînement · séances · programme · soutenir · aider à · client · évaluation · cartographie · non invasif et indolore).

### **Never do**

Hype · fear-selling · clinical coldness · vague wellness language · naming a condition we claim to address · implying an outcome timeline · emojis beyond an occasional single functional one.

## ---

**4\. Narrative mode — long-form only**

Applies to blog posts, pitches, executive summaries, decks, webinar scripts. Not to captions, review replies or GBP posts.

> * Open with history, roots, origins or etymology. Wander back in time before going forward.  
> * Arc: past → present → future.  
> * Metaphors over mechanics. Principles before examples. (Compatible with “mechanism before benefit”: the mechanism comes first, told through a metaphor.)  
> * Close with an open, optimistic or suspenseful look forward. Never a summary. Never a risk table.

## ---

**5\. Language**

Master language: English. Output French, German, English or Luxembourgish on request.

> * French is the default for local client-facing content.  
> * Detect the language of an incoming review and reply in that language. Luxembourgish → reply in French unless the reviewer signals otherwise.  
> * Never machine-translate idiom. Re-express it natively.  
> * Technical neuroscience terminology stays accurate in every language.  
> * German runs roughly 30% longer than English — never rely on tight character counts.

### **Hard rules**

> 1. This is a light-mode brand. White ground, ink text, lilac bands, plum/blue for depth.  
> 2. Body text is \#090909 on \#ffffff. Never a brand colour.  
> 3. \#9f016b is links and accents — not paragraphs.  
> 4. \#e2d4e2 is a surface, never text on white.  
> 5. The gradient always carries white text.  
> 6. Never introduce a colour not listed above.  
> 7. Focus ring 0 0 0 3px rgba(159,1,107,0.45) — never removed.  
> 8. prefers-reduced-motion disables all decorative motion.  
> 9. Spacing only from 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128\.  
> 10. One data callout per view.

### **Typography**

> * Myriad Pro — Canva, print, internal design. Adobe-licensed, never ships to the browser.  
> * Source Sans 3 — the web font. Adobe's own open humanist sans, same lineage, free and self-hostable.  
> * No font licensing will be purchased. Decided 2026-08-01. Do not reopen.

### **Imagery (new in v1.3)**

> * **Mood:** calm, competent, warm, human-centred, modern European practice — light and bright, never sterile.  
> * **Prefer real over AI:** real equipment, real cap setups, our real offices, natural daylight, natural skin tones.  
> * **Preferred subjects:** a relaxed adult in a comfortable chair during a session; calm interiors; EEG waveforms and brain visuals in plum and slate (scientific-illustration style, not sci-fi); diverse adults of all ages.  
> * **Motif:** the low-poly brain of the logo; optional EEG trace from irregular slow waves to an alpha spindle — never an ECG heartbeat, never presented as a real client's data.  
> * **Never:** hospitals, surgery, cold fluorescent light; before/after comparisons; people in distress; children in medical settings; miracle or dramatic transformation visuals; stock “woman meditating on a beach”; glowing generic brains; lens-flare pseudo-science; lightbulb-idea clichés; other companies' logos or equipment (recoveriX materials excepted); AI-generated images of real team members or clients.  
> * AI-generated images carry an AI label (EU AI Act Art. 50).

### **Reject on sight**

Any dark “Clinical Dark-Mode” theme · \#1A1A2E · \#16213E · \#3A86FF · \#C73380 · \#1B3A5C · \#0D9488 · \#E8614D · Montserrat or Inter as a brand font · any claim that we are located in Mersch.

“Optimize Your Brain, Improve Your Life\!” · “Take charge of your mental health” · « Prenez en main votre santé mentale ». Retired: “Train your brain. Find your balance.” (and its FR/DE versions).
