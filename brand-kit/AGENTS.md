# Brand kit — rules for every agent

Applies to Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Hermes and Antigravity (agy) for any Neurofeedback Luxembourg, recoveriX Luxembourg or Brain-Curator output.

1. **Check `VERSION` first.** Before any brand work, read `brand-kit/VERSION` in `Neurofeedback-Luxembourg/brand-guide` (main). If the version you loaded or pinned is different, stop and say so.
2. **Follow `brand-guide.md`** (Brand Guide v2.1.3, approved 2 October 2026, generated from the Google Doc), the only rulebook. §11 states visual identity, voice and languages. It wins every conflict. The `skill/` folder is the same rules as a loadable skill (`nfl-brand`).
3. **Use `tokens/`** (`tokens.json`, `tokens.css`) for every colour, font, spacing step and radius. Light mode only.
4. **Never invent figures.** Statistics and research numbers come only from the data file named in `figures.md` or the primary source it cites, with source and year. Authority figures, phone numbers, prices and business facts come only from `brand-guide.md` and `skill/references/business-facts.md`.
5. **Never redraw logos.** Use the files in `logos/` as they are.
6. **Review with `tests/`** before anything is published: the 12 brand tests are the checklist.

Do not keep a private copy of these rules. Read them from this repo, or pin a VERSION and re-check it.
