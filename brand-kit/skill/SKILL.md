# Neurofeedback Luxembourg — brand (Brand Guide v2.1.3, skill v2.1.5)

Generated from `Neurofeedback-Luxembourg/brand-guide` → `guide/brand-guide.md` (Google Doc 1NxZxOLLb2euoepbsEsnt7I4n8vNK-BRg4JSurg7MXrE). The brand kit (`brand-kit/` in that repo: VERSION, tokens, logos, figures pointer, 12 tests) is the shared source for every agent. Do not edit this skill by hand; change the guide and regenerate. Brand Lock v1.3 is SUPERSEDED.

1. Read `references/brand-guide.md` first — it wins every conflict. Visual identity, voice and languages are stated in Brand Guide v2.1.3 §11. Address, hours, prices, team and client pathway come only from `references/business-facts.md`; anything it lists as not confirmed (device prices…) must not be stated. Never mention discontinued services (HRV assessment, EEG-assisted meditation, NeuroCoaching, "Brain Performance Enhancement").
2. Three fixed rules, never broken: never invent (every number carries its source) · never promise an outcome (no "cure", "guaranteed", "100% safe", "no side effects", "miracle") · protect clients (no identifying details or health details without written consent). Everything else is guidance.
3. Label every statement with the claim ladder: **Fact** (source, sample, date) · **Research finding** (study + design: RCT, sham, uncontrolled…) · **Our observation** (own data, method, period) · **Opinion** (signed, first person).
4. Authority figures, always exactly: 3,500+ brain maps · 25,000+ sessions · since 2014 · 19 electrodes · 140+ Google reviews, 4.9/5 average (never "five-star"). Use as many numbers as the piece needs, each sourced.
5. Pick the tone from the channel table (§8). No fake scarcity or countdowns anywhere.
6. Labels: AI-generated images carry an AI label (EU AI Act Art. 50). Health content ends with the label in its language — EN "Neurofeedback is brain training, not a medical treatment, and does not replace medical advice." · FR « Le neurofeedback est un entraînement cérébral, pas un traitement médical, et ne remplace pas un avis médical. » · DE „Neurofeedback ist ein Gehirntraining, keine medizinische Behandlung, und ersetzt keine ärztliche Beratung." recoveriX content uses g.tec's intended-purpose wording instead (rule 10).
6b. Signed long-form pieces only (opinion pieces, articles, pitches, executive summaries, presentations): open with history, roots or etymology; past → present → future; metaphors over mechanics; end with an open, optimistic or suspenseful look forward, never a summary or risk table. Not for the statistics page, service pages or social posts.
7. Clients: never identify a client (including a reviewer, where naming them would reveal they are a client) or repeat their health details without written consent; client stories only with consent, labelled "Our observation".
8. Visual assets (slides, social posts, one-pagers, HTML, PPTX, PNG): use `assets/brand.css` tokens and start from `assets/template.html`. Use the real logo files — never trace, redraw or generate a substitute (the planned official SVG redraw is the only exception, once approved): `assets/logo-white.png` (white badge, top-right, on the gradient), `assets/logo-transparent.png` (on white), `assets/logo-reversed-transparent.png` (white text, on dark/gradient without a badge), `assets/logo-icon-transparent.png` (icon only, small formats). Embed the logo as a base64 data URI in HTML so the file stands alone. Light mode only, gradient always with white text, magenta #9f016b for links, accents and active states — never paragraphs, Source Sans 3.
9. At the end of every answer, list anything you could not source or are unsure about. He approves templates and opinion pieces; content built from an approved template using only numbers already on the statistics page needs no per-piece review.
10. recoveriX: use g.tec's intended purpose word for word, never a paraphrase and never a wider claim: "recoveriX PRO is intended • for muscle re-education by powered muscle stimulation and • for neurofeedback." Indication for use, also word for word when needed: "The indication for use of recoveriX PRO for muscle stimulation is given when the extensor digitorum communis muscles of the lower arms that are partly or fully paralyzed due to stroke should be contracted to generate movement." Source: g.tec medical engineering GmbH, recoveriX PRO Instructions for Use, V2.18.01 (Rev. 2.6), 2021, §2.1–2.2 — Google Drive https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CSYNFKFmbVPCCHnlEiY6vEZyOblXLAxW/view. If a newer Instructions for Use exists, its wording wins.
11. Statistics and figures in any content come only from the statistics data file (`references/statistics-figures.json`, master copy in GitHub Neurofeedback-Luxembourg/nfl-web `src/data/figures.json`) or from the primary source it cites — never from press articles or secondary summaries. Quote the exact number and year as in the data file, with the comparison year wherever the data file has one (e.g. "36% in 2025, up from 16% in 2014"), and keep its population and period.
12. Service pages stay calm and reassuring. Research limitations, negative and null results belong on the statistics page (data.neurofeedback-luxembourg.com), which service pages link to; they are not argued in the service-page text itself.
13. Never leave citation fragments, footnote numbers, "accessed on" dates or source domain names (e.g. "who.int", "pubmed") in the copy itself; name the source in plain words or link it.

## Standing rules for drafting
Apply the brand guide as standing instructions, not just as a source to read.
Every health-related piece ends with the exact §1 disclaimer in the language of the piece:
- EN: "Neurofeedback is brain training, not a medical treatment, and does not replace medical advice."
- FR: « Le neurofeedback est un entraînement cérébral, pas un traitement médical, et ne remplace pas un avis médical. »
- DE: „Neurofeedback ist ein Gehirntraining, keine medizinische Behandlung, und ersetzt keine ärztliche Beratung."
For recoveriX, use g.tec's intended-purpose wording instead, as §1 requires.
Every number carries its source; mark unconfirmed dates as unconfirmed.
Write missing links as a visible placeholder, never invent them.
